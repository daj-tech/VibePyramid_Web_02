ABOUT LOCALLINK

Connecting people with useful local information.

LocalLink is a web-based community information platform designed to make locally relevant information easier to discover and understand.

Get Started
THE PROBLEM

Useful community information is often scattered.

Information about internships, lost items, emergencies, local issues, events, scholarships and important announcements may be distributed through WhatsApp groups, conversations and personal networks.

💬

Information gets lost in conversations.

When important local information is shared across different conversations and networks, people may have difficulty discovering it later or knowing whether it is still relevant.

01
Difficult discovery

Useful information can be difficult to find when needed.

02
Local relevance

People need information relevant to their local area.

03
Changing information

Events, deadlines and local issues can become outdated or resolved.

OUR SOLUTION

One organized place for local information.

LocalLink organizes locally relevant information into structured categories and allows users to discover information according to their selected area.

📍

Local Discovery

Information can be viewed according to the selected local area.

🗂️

Structured Categories

Information is organized into six clear categories for easier discovery.

📅

Calendar

Important events and deadlines can be viewed through a unified calendar.

🔔

Reminders

The platform is designed to support PWA and browser notifications for important dates.

INFORMATION LIFECYCLE

Information is designed to have a lifecycle.

The LocalLink concept follows the flow defined for the problem statement.

01 Create Information Prepare locally relevant information.
→
02 Publish Make the information available.
→
03 Discover Find relevant information.
→
04 Evaluate Consider relevance and validity.
→
05 Update / Report Maintain or report information.
→
06 Expire / Resolve Close outdated or completed information.
STATUS SYSTEM

Understand the current state of information.

LocalLink uses clear status labels to make information easier to understand.

●
ACTIVE

Information that is currently relevant.

◷
UPCOMING

Information connected to a future date.

○
EXPIRED

Information whose validity or deadline has ended.

✓
RESOLVED

Information about an issue that has been completed.
