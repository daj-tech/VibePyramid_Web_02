HOW LOCALLINK WORKS

From information to useful local discovery.

The platform is designed around the complete information lifecycle defined for the LocalLink solution.

01

Create

Local information is prepared.

02

Publish

Information becomes available.

03

Discover

Users find relevant information.

04

Evaluate

Relevance and validity are considered.

05

Update / Report

Information can be maintained.

06

Expire / Resolve

Outdated or completed information is closed.
